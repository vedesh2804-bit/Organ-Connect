ABOUT THE PROJECT

Technology with a human purpose.

OrganConnect is a front-end academic prototype created to demonstrate how a clean digital experience can support organ donation awareness.

✳

Our concept

Make awareness information easy to explore, demonstrate a sample donor directory, and explain the importance of official channels.

⌘

Built with

HTML5 for structure, CSS3 for responsive styling, and JavaScript for form validation, filtering and browser-based demo storage.

♡

Our responsibility

Real organ donation and transplantation are regulated medical processes. This prototype does not provide medical advice or donor matching.

PROJECT SUPPORT

Questions about the demo?

For academic feedback, contact your project team or faculty guide. Do not submit personal health information through this prototype.
